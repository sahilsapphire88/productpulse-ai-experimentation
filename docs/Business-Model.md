# Business Model — ExperimentLab

> **ILLUSTRATIVE** — Pricing and tiers are hypothetical and for portfolio purposes only.

## Pricing Tiers

### Free — $0/month
- Limited experiments/month (illustrative: 5)
- Basic hypothesis generation
- Basic analysis
- Synthetic demo data

### Pro — $49/month (illustrative)
- Unlimited experiments
- Advanced analytics (confidence intervals, guardrails)
- AI assistant
- AI cost optimization
- What-if simulator

### Team — $149/month per seat (illustrative)
- All Pro features
- Collaboration
- Shared experiment library
- Advanced reporting
- Decision log with team visibility

## Value Proposition

- PMs make faster, evidence-based decisions.
- AI PMs optimize inference cost vs product impact.
- Teams reduce wasted engineering effort on features that shouldn't ship.

## Ethical Label

All pricing assumptions are **Demo / Illustrative**. Not real pricing.
