# User Research Plan — ExperimentLab

> **ILLUSTRATIVE** — This research plan is hypothetical. No real interviews have been conducted. Any example participants are labelled **Illustrative Research Participant**.

## Objectives

Understand how Product Managers design, run, and make decisions from experiments, and where ExperimentLab can add value.

## Research Questions

1. How do PMs currently design experiments?
2. What makes experimentation difficult?
3. How do PMs decide when evidence is sufficient to ship?
4. How do teams handle inconclusive results?
5. How important is AI/infrastructure cost in the decision?
6. What causes experiment analysis delays?

## Methodology

- Semi-structured interviews with PMs, Growth PMs, AI PMs, and Product Analysts.
- No fabrication of interview data. All participants are illustrative.
