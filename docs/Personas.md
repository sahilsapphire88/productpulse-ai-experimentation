# Personas — ExperimentLab

> **ILLUSTRATIVE** — Personas are hypothetical and for product design purposes only.

## 1. Product Manager (Sarah)

**Goal:** Validate whether a feature should ship.  
**Pain points:** Doesn't know which metric to prioritize; unsure if lift is statistically significant; doesn't factor AI cost into decisions.  
**Needs:** Clear hypothesis, statistical significance, SHIP/ITERATE/KILL recommendation with reasoning.

## 2. Growth Product Manager (Marcus)

**Goal:** Run experiments around activation, conversion, retention, engagement.  
**Pain points:** Experiments take too long to analyze; guardrail metrics are often ignored; traffic allocation is hard to optimize.  
**Needs:** Experiment designer, guardrail monitoring, what-if simulator for traffic allocation.

## 3. AI Product Manager (Elena)

**Goal:** Evaluate both product impact and AI/inference cost.  
**Pain points:** Inference cost is invisible in most A/B tools; doesn't know if lift justifies the cost.  
**Needs:** AI cost analysis, incremental revenue vs incremental cost, ROI, cost per conversion.

## 4. Product Analyst (David)

**Goal:** Analyze experiment results and identify meaningful patterns.  
**Pain points:** Data is spread across tools; statistical rigor varies; hard to communicate decisions to stakeholders.  
**Needs:** Analysis engine with confidence intervals, decision log, exportable results.
