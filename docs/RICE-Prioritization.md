# RICE Prioritization — ExperimentLab

> **ILLUSTRATIVE** — RICE scores are demo assumptions for portfolio purposes.

## Formula

**RICE = Reach × Impact × Confidence / Effort**

- Reach: # of users affected per quarter
- Impact: 1 (minimal) to 3 (massive)
- Confidence: % (50%, 80%, 100%)
- Effort: person-weeks
